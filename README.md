# UK Theory Driving Test — Support

Support page for **UK Theory Driving Test**, an iOS app to help you prepare for the UK driving theory test by understanding the rules behind each answer.

This app is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the DVSA. Traffic sign designs are Crown copyright, used under the Open Government Licence.

## Features

- 📝 Practice questions with explained answers
- 🔁 Review Due (spaced repetition) and Weak Areas to focus your revision
- 🎯 15 fixed mock papers: 50 questions, 57-minute limit, 43/50 pass mark
- ⚠️ Hazard-awareness practice using still photographs (unofficial; not the DVSA video test)
- 🚦 UK road signs library with sign practice
- 💡 Rule-based tutor that explains topics using written study content (not a generative AI chat service)
- ⭐ Premium: unlimited practice, mock exams and tutor prompts, plus advanced statistics and a study plan

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I restore my Premium subscription on a new device?**
Open the app and use Restore Purchases on the Premium screen, signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select UK Theory Driving Test, and choose Cancel Subscription.

**How do I delete my account and data?**
Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account in the app. This removes the local credentials and study data on your device. It does not cancel an Apple subscription.
