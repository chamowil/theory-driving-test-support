# Theory Driving Test — Support

Support page for **Theory Driving Test**, an iOS app to help you prepare for and pass the UK DVSA car theory test.

## Features

- 📝 Practice questions from the official DVSA question bank
- 🎯 Full-length mock exams that mirror the real test format
- ⚠️ Hazard Perception practice clips
- 🚦 Road signs library
- 🤖 AI Tutor to explain tricky topics
- 📊 Statistics to track your progress
- ⭐ Premium: unlimited mock exams, AI Tutor, and advanced statistics

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I restore my Premium subscription on a new device?**
Open the app, go to Settings → Restore Purchases, and sign in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select Theory Driving Test, and choose Cancel Subscription.

**How do I delete my account and data?**
Email us at the address above and we'll delete your account data within 30 days, or use the in-app Delete Account option in Settings if available.
